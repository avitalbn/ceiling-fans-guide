4. Before you approve the order

  • Measure each room, ceiling height and fan position. Have the installer confirm blade clearances and structural support, especially near bunk beds, cupboards and beams.
  • Ask for a seven-fan, itemised installed quote: exact SKU, finish, blade diameter, light kit, rod length, remote, shipping and installation.
  • Confirm outdoor exposure, rain and coastal corrosion coverage in writing. An IP rating alone is not a complete corrosion warranty.
  • For bedrooms, request a low-speed demonstration. Do not treat promotional videos as measured noise comparisons.
  • Resolve Nexus kit SKU 85125 and the long rod SKU 85177. Confirm actual rod length, finish, connectors and suitability for your ceiling.
  • Wave remote/receiver SKU 85111 has quantity zero in the quote. Ask whether you want wall controls or remotes for both pergola fans.
  • Check lead times against construction completion, spare LED/remote availability and when warranty coverage starts.

Suggested negotiating position

Compare Talia for the three kids' rooms; retain the parents' Diamond until sizing and noise are evaluated; compare outdoor DC alternatives only after allowing for lighting; retain Nexus as a design candidate subject to compatibility confirmation.
